📋 IMPLEMENTATION OVERVIEW

When multiple passengers share the same ride but have different starting and
ending points, each passenger's fare is now calculated based on their
individual journey segment.

📊 FARE CALCULATION FORMULA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Total Fare = 20 (base) + (distance_km × 8) + (duration_min × 0.5)

Example: 15km, 45min ride with 3 participants
  Total Fare = 20 + (15 × 8) + (45 × 0.5) = 162.50 BDT
  Initiator pays: 81.25 BDT (50%)
  Each passenger pays: 40.63 BDT (25% each)
